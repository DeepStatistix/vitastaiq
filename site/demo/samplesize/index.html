Before you rely on this

  • These are standard normal-approximation formulas (Chow, Shao & Wang; Fleiss; Lwanga & Lemeshow) with a small-sample correction for means. Results agree with commonly published values, for example 64 per group for a medium effect (d = 0.5) at 80% power and 5% two-sided alpha.
  • The inputs are your assumptions, such as the standard deviation and the smallest difference worth detecting. Take them from a pilot study or published papers, and cite the source in your protocol.
  • Complex designs (clustered, survival, repeated measures, multiple endpoints, non-inferiority) need a different calculation. Ask a statistician before you finalise.
