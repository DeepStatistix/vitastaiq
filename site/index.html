Questions

        
Good to know

        

          
Will you write my thesis or paper?
No. We help with study design, analysis and interpretation of your own real data, and we teach you the results so you can defend them. The writing and the conclusions stay yours.

          
How is pricing decided?
Every project gets a fixed quote after a short scoping chat, based on the data, the deliverables and the deadline. Students and early-stage teams are welcome to ask about reduced rates.

          
Is my data kept confidential?
Yes. Client data is used only for your project and is not shared or published. We can sign a simple confidentiality agreement on request.

          
Which software do you use?
SPSS, R and Python for analysis; Power BI, Tableau or web dashboards for reporting. We deliver in the tools you can open and maintain.

          
Can you work with clients outside Kashmir?
Yes. Most work is remote, by email, calls and shared files.

          
How fast can you start?
Small analyses can start within days. Larger builds are scheduled after the scope is agreed.
