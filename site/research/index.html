Computational Economics · 2025 · sole author

A hybrid ensemble for stock price prediction

Bagging, boosting, dagging and stacking combined through a meta-learner. On the chronological test set it had MSE 53.4, against 75.7, 149.3 and 171.9 for the single strategies.

AgroDesign: design-aware statistical inference

You declare the experimental design (CRD, RCBD, factorial, split-plot, multi-environment). AgroDesign builds the correct model and error strata, then runs ANOVA, mean separation, mixed models and G×E stability.
