Our line

What we do

  • Design, analyse and interpret your real data
  • Teach you the method so you can explain and defend it
  • Document every step so your work is reproducible
  • Keep your data confidential

What we never do

  • Write your thesis or conclusions for you
  • Invent, alter or remove data to get a result
  • Hide results that disagree with your hypothesis
  • Promise a "significant" p-value

Statistical support is a normal part of research, but rules differ between universities. Please check your institution's policy on external help and acknowledge our contribution if required.

Keeping your data safe

Never send patient names, phone numbers, addresses or hospital IDs. Share only coded data, where each participant is a number, with no way to identify them. Do not send identifiable data by email or message. We will agree how to share your dataset before you send anything.

Questions students ask

Will you write my thesis?

No. We help with design, analysis and interpretation of your own data and teach you the results, so the writing, discussion and conclusions stay yours.

Can you help if my data is already collected?

Yes. Send a description of your study and variables. We check what analysis your data can support and tell you honestly if there are problems, such as a sample that is too small for your question.

Which software do you use?

SPSS or R, depending on what you or your department prefer. You receive the outputs and a plain explanation either way.

How is the price decided?

After a short scoping conversation we give a written, fixed quote based on your data, the analysis needed and your deadline. Students are welcome to ask about options that fit a limited budget.

How soon can you start?

Small questions can often be answered within days. For full analyses, share your deadline in the form and we will agree a timeline in writing. Contact us early if you have a hard submission date.

Will you help me answer examiners or reviewers?

Yes. We can review statistical comments, check what needs re-analysis and explain clearly what to write in your reply.
